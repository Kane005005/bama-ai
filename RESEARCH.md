# Research Log

This document is the high-level research index for Bama AI.

## Research questions

### Language
- What exactly do we mean by Bamanankan in this project?
- Which orthographic conventions will we support?
- How should dialectal variation be represented?
- Which linguistic resources are authoritative for specific tasks?

### Speech
- What Bamanankan speech datasets already exist?
- What are their licenses and collection conditions?
- Which STT architectures work well for low-resource languages?
- How much transcribed speech is needed for useful baselines?
- How should pronunciation and prosody be evaluated for TTS?

### Models
- Which open models already support Bambara/Bamanankan?
- Can existing multilingual models be adapted efficiently?
- What are the trade-offs between fine-tuning, adapters, continued pretraining and training from scratch?
- Where is French or English being used as an unwanted intermediate representation?

### Evaluation
- Which benchmarks reflect real Bamanankan use?
- How do we evaluate semantic understanding rather than translation quality?
- How do native speakers judge naturalness and cultural appropriateness?

### Ethics and governance
- How should voice consent be obtained?
- What metadata is safe and useful?
- How do contributors withdraw voice data where applicable?
- Which datasets may be redistributed?

## Research standard

For each significant experiment, record:

1. Question
2. Hypothesis
3. Data
4. Model/system
5. Configuration
6. Evaluation method
7. Results
8. Failure cases
9. Interpretation
10. Next action

## Evidence levels

- **Observed:** directly measured.
- **Documented:** supported by a cited external source.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible but not established.
- **Decision:** project choice based on available evidence.

Do not present hypotheses as facts.
